Privacy policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Docklume is a macOS app that mounts online storage (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, S3, NAS…) as disks in the Finder, using the open-source rclone. It runs entirely on your Mac.

What Docklume does not do

  • Docklume collects no personal data and has no server.
  • No data is sent to the app's author or to third parties.
  • The app contains no telemetry, usage statistics or advertising.

Data from connected services

When you connect a service, Google Drive for example, you authorize Docklume in your browser. The service then issues an access token, which rclone saves in its configuration file on your Mac (~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf).

This token is used only to:

  • list, read and, if you turn read-only off, create, change and delete your files, at your request, through the mounted drive;
  • show the account's email address and the available space in the app.

Files read or waiting to be uploaded may be kept for a while in a cache on your Mac (~/Library/Caches/Docklume). You can clear it at any time from the app. Data travels directly between your Mac and the service's servers, over HTTPS.

Google user data

Docklume's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is not sold, not used for advertising and not read by anyone.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting a drive in Docklume erases its cache and, if it was created in the app, its rclone configuration.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your account, for example at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google.

Contact

For any question, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
